How I Approach Engineering

  • 01

    Latency is a core feature. Profiling execution plans and designing covered indexes consistently delivers more value than throwing raw hardware at slow queries.

  • 02

    Decouple by default. Replaced brittle batch synchronizations with asynchronous Kafka event pipelines to isolate microservice failures and maintain zero lag.

  • 03

    Local-first where privacy matters. In tools like Porcupine, ran ONNX transformer embeddings and vector similarity entirely on local desktop CPUs without cloud leakage.

  • 04

    Measure with metrics, not assumptions. From maintaining 85%+ JUnit/Mockito test coverage to load-testing TopoJSON rendering on low-spec hardware.
